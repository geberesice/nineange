# Nineange

A game designed by Batu, built with his dad.

**Play it here: https://geberesice.github.io/nineange/**

You walk around a forest, find an egg, and hatch a pet that follows you
everywhere. Sometimes it's a dragon and sometimes it's a llama — its shell
gives you a clue before it hatches. Feed it, give it water, play with it, and
watch it grow. Four eggs are hidden, in a different place every time you play.

East across the bridge is Starter Town, where thirty-two people live. Talk to
any of them, run errands for coins, buy food and gear at the stalls, sleep at
the inn, and take a poorly pet to the vet. What you wear and what your pet
wears both count in a fight.

## Playing

Open the link on a phone, tablet, laptop or Chromebook. There is nothing to
install and no account to make. Your game saves itself in your own browser, so
the pet you hatch is yours.

**Move** with the arrow keys or WASD. **E** to eat, **Q** to drink, **F** to
play with your pet, **B** for your bag, **Space** for whatever is beside you.
On a tablet every one of those is an on-screen button instead, and everything
in a conversation or a shop can be tapped.

Walk over an egg to pick it up, then keep walking — it hatches after a
hundred steps and follows you from then on.

This page holds just the built game. The source lives in a private
repository.
